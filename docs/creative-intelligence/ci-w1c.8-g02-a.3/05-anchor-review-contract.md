# Anchor Review Contract

The review is a manual source-to-claim-to-anchor comparison under the zero-model qualification boundary. The reviewer role is `independent-qualification-design-reviewer`.

Required review dimensions are Source Fidelity, Material Coverage, Epistemic Discipline, Independence, and Traceability. Each applicable dimension must score at least 2 and the applicable average must be at least 2.4. This A.3 map records 3/3/3/3/3, average 3.0.

The anchor epistemic expectation enum is exactly `FACT`, `USER_REQUIREMENT`, `MIXED`, `OPEN`:

- `MIXED` is used where source-defined intent coexists with unverified performance or category interpretation;
- `OPEN` means the reviewer does not pre-authorize the eventual runtime epistemic class;
- runtime Planning epistemic classification remains authoritative;
- `UNKNOWN` is not used as an anchor expectation value.

Review PASS does not validate medical efficacy, regulatory approval, financial feasibility, valuation, or external market data.
