# Brand Poster Template

Template Version: 1.0.0

## 1. Task Definition

Create one complete campaign-ready brand poster with a clear communication event, strong visual anchor, and professional presentation value.

## 2. Brand Context

Use `visualMemory.brand_core`: positioning, industry, mood, and core temperament.

## 3. Visual Mechanism

Turn the approved graphic language into one repeatable campaign mechanism rather than rearranging the previous poster.

## 4. Color System

Establish a dominant field, a supporting identity layer, and an accent layer capped at 10%. Maintain contrast suitable for the communication hierarchy.

## 5. Material System

Describe image texture, print or screen behavior, surface depth, and any tactile or photographic material cues required by the visual memory.

## 6. Composition / Structure

Use one dominant visual, a clear information hierarchy, intentional negative space, readable near/far rhythm, and a reserved copy area.

## 7. Photography Direction

Choose one credible commercial photography or graphic viewpoint, controlled lighting, purposeful crop, and a full-bleed poster presentation.

## 8. Negative Rules

No poster collections, mockup scenes, portfolio boards, random text blocks, Logo-plus-product templates, or copies of the previous poster composition.
