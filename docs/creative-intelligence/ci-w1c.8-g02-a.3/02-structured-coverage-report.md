# Structured Coverage Report

Local OOXML parsing produced 7,363 normalized characters, one parser section, 39 numbered logical sections, and four tables. The extraction schema was not changed.

All eight deterministic strategic domains are covered: audience/customer, business model, strategic objective, competition/differentiation, product/service, channel/go-to-market, growth/transformation, and funding/finance. Coverage is therefore 8/8, ratio 1.0.

The ratio measures domain presence, not truth completeness. Diagnostic gaps remain:

- audited historical financials;
- unit economics and acquisition/retention assumptions;
- concrete regulatory and approval status;
- technical and clinical validation of AI/performance claims;
- named execution owners and milestone acceptance criteria.

These gaps remain explicit diagnostics and are not silently converted into hard qualification gates.
