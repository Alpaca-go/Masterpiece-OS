## What counts as PLANNING_STRATEGIC_SOURCE (positive planning)

A real planning strategic source must contain AT LEAST ONE of:
- brand positioning (formal written statement)
- business / service strategy (offerings, market, business model)
- audience definition (demographics, personas, segments)
- brand promise (value proposition)
- competitive context (market positioning, competitor analysis)
- communication task (what the brand needs to communicate)
- strategic objective (business goals tied to brand)
- experience objective (brand experience goals)
- transformation objective (the change the brand seeks)

A file is a PLANNING_STRATEGIC_SOURCE if it is a human-authored planning document (brief, brand strategy doc, positioning document, market research, etc.) — NOT a visual asset, NOT a model-generated diagnosis, NOT a CI shadow output.

- **CI-W1C.7.3 reading**: "13-14 of 15-16 project-specific planning anchors are LOST between DI/DVC and Evidence. The first-loss stage is later in the pipeline."
