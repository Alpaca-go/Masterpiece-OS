# Qualification Review Applicability Contract

## Stage-scoped applicability

Strategic-only qualification has five applicable dimensions:

- Planning Fidelity;
- Strategic Specificity;
- Semantic Retention;
- Insight Quality;
- Traceability.

Because Concept and Direction are not authorized, Conceptual Distinctness and Visual Discussability are recorded as `N/A — stage not authorized`. They are retained in the rubric but excluded from the hard minimum and average denominator.

When Concept is authorized, Conceptual Distinctness becomes applicable. When Direction is authorized, Visual Discussability becomes applicable.

## Frozen thresholds

- every applicable dimension >= 2;
- applicable average >= 2.4;
- Planning Fidelity >= 2;
- Strategic Specificity >= 2;
- Traceability >= 2.

No threshold was reduced. A pure `evaluateQualificationReview` helper now makes applicability and denominator behavior executable and testable.

## Historical boundary

R1.3 Attempt 2 remains `HOLD_FOR_TRACEABILITY_REPAIR` with its original recorded scores. This contract is prospective and does not rewrite the historical review.
