# Packaging Render Template

Template Version: 1.0.0

## 1. Task Definition

Create one production-credible packaging hero render that communicates structure, product category, material quality, and commercial shelf value.

## 2. Brand Context

Use `visualMemory.brand_core`: positioning, industry, mood, and core temperament.

## 3. Visual Mechanism

Apply the approved graphic language to the packaging structure and information hierarchy without copying the old pack layout.

## 4. Color System

Establish a dominant pack field, a supporting identity layer, and an accent layer capped at 10%. Preserve approved brand relationships and reject prohibited colors.

## 5. Material System

Describe substrate, finish, print process, edge behavior, opening mechanism, tactility, and how light reacts to the surface.

## 6. Composition / Structure

Show one primary package, its real structure, opening relationship, scale, product placement, and a controlled three-quarter product view.

## 7. Photography Direction

Use premium commercial product photography, accurate perspective, controlled highlights, readable edges, and believable contact shadows.

## 8. Negative Rules

No packaging collections, dieline collages, proposal boards, random placeholder text, material-only reskins, or copies of the previous packaging layout.
