# G02 Source-Selection Contract

R1.7 selects no G02 file and reads no G02 source. Future selection belongs to a separately authorized phase.

## Selection criteria

A candidate should materially differ from G01 across several of these dimensions:

- industry or category;
- document structure and writing style;
- claim density and requirement modality;
- audience model and business model;
- strategy vocabulary and proposition structure.

The aim is to test generalization, not similarity to G01. Selection must establish one exact source boundary, calculate its identity without scanning sibling or parent content, and record why it is independent.

## Future acceptance record

Before any qualification design can advance to live authorization, the future source record must contain a repository-safe display name, exact source SHA-256, registered content hash if applicable, document role, selection rationale, boundary proof, and reviewer approval. Machine-local absolute paths and credentials must never enter tracked artifacts.

This contract does not authorize source selection, source reading, external scanning, or live execution.
