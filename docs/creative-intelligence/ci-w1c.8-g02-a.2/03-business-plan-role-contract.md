# Business Plan Role Contract

`business-plan` means a document whose primary information architecture plans a business across multiple strategic domains. It is not an alias for `brand-strategy`.

Role evidence may come from a strong business-plan/proposal identity, repeated business-plan body signals, or a weak `BP` identity combined with at least four strategic domains. Eligibility always requires four or more strategic domains, regardless of filename.

Business-plan content may carry `brand-strategy`, `market-research`, or `product-information` as secondary roles. Those secondaries preserve the mixed nature of the document without changing its primary role. Sparse, non-strategic, filename-only `BP` input remains `unknown` and ineligible.
