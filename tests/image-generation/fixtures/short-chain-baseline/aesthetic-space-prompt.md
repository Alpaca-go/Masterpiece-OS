# Sanitized successful reception-space baseline

## Deliverable

Create one formal, photorealistic reception-space rendering. It must depict a
real, enterable interior rather than a VI board, material board, moodboard, or
portfolio layout.

## Functional structure

Show the entrance relationship, reception counter, waiting area, circulation,
floor, walls, ceiling, openings, and believable human scale.

## Project identity slots

Use only the current project's confirmed identity, color behavior, material
behavior, lighting behavior, and locked assets. Do not import visual answers
from another project.

## Spatial translation

Translate brand identity into architectural proportion, material junctions,
light, rhythm, signage placement, and operational details. Do not paste a logo
or flat graphic across surfaces as a substitute for spatial design.

## Camera

Entrance three-quarter wide view, eye-level commercial interior photography,
16:9, complete spatial depth, physically believable perspective.

## Negative constraints

No VI application collection, stationery, packaging lineup, swatch board,
floating poster, unexplained paper mockup, multi-panel presentation, or text
describing the design.
