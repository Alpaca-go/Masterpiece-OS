# Anchor Epistemic Expectation Contract

The canonical G02 anchor expectation enum is exactly:

`FACT`, `USER_REQUIREMENT`, `MIXED`, `OPEN`.

`OPEN` means the human reviewer deliberately does not pre-judge the runtime classification. It is non-authoritative. The deterministic Planning epistemic classifier remains final authority at runtime. `UNKNOWN` is not an anchor expectation value and therefore cannot be confused with “reviewer left open.”

This phase defines and verifies the generic contract only. It does not construct, restore, or review a G02 Anchor Map.
